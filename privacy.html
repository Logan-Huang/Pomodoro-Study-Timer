Privacy Policy

Effective October 1, 2026 · Last updated October 1, 2026

The short version

  • Aura works fully without an account. Your tasks, stats and settings then stay in your browser and are never sent to us.
  • If you choose to sign in, we store your email address and the data you sync (tasks, focus sessions, settings, timer) with Google Firebase so it’s available on your other devices.
  • Your Google Calendar data is processed only in your browser and is never uploaded to our servers.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking cookies, and we never sell or share your data.
  • You can export or permanently delete your data at any time from Settings.

1. Who we are

Aura (“Aura”, “we”, “us”) is a free study-timer web app made by an independent developer, Logan Huang, and published at logan-huang.github.io/Pomodoro-Study-Timer. We are the “controller” of the account data described below. Questions? See Contact.

2. What Aura collects

a. If you don’t sign in (the default)

Aura saves your settings, tasks, focus-session history and timer state in your browser’s local storage on your device so the app remembers them. This information stays on your device. We can’t see it, and it isn’t sent to us or to Firebase.

c. Google Calendar (optional)

If you connect Google Calendar, Aura asks Google for read-only access to your calendar. It asks for write access only if you turn on “Log focus sessions to Google Calendar”. Your calendar events are fetched and processed only in your browser to show your schedule and plan study sessions. A copy is cached in your browser’s storage. Calendar events and Google access tokens are never sent to Aura’s servers or to Firebase, never shared, and never used for anything other than these in-app features. Your calendar selection is stored only on each device, and when a focus plan for a calendar event syncs to your other devices, it carries only its end time, not the event’s name or ID. If you turn on session logging, Aura adds an event such as “Focus · your task” to your primary calendar, directly from your browser, after each focus session. Access tokens expire after about an hour, and you can disconnect at any time from the Schedule menu or at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Aura’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

d. What we don’t collect

  • No analytics, advertising, tracking pixels, fingerprinting or third-party cookies.
  • No payment information. Aura is free.
  • No contacts, location, microphone or camera access. All sounds are generated on your device.

3. How we use your data

Only to provide Aura’s features you ask for (sign-in and sync across devices) and to keep the service secure. We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, selling, or training AI models, and we do not read your tasks except where you ask us to help with your account.

4. Legal bases (EEA/UK users)

  • Contract: storing and syncing your account data is necessary to provide the account service you requested.
  • Consent: connecting Google Calendar. You can withdraw consent at any time by disconnecting.
  • Legitimate interests: keeping the service secure and preventing abuse.

6. International transfers

Account data is stored and processed in the United States. If you use Aura from outside the US, your data is transferred there. Google provides contractual safeguards for such transfers, including Standard Contractual Clauses.

7. How long we keep data

  • Account data is kept while your account exists. When you delete your account in Settings, your synced data is deleted from our database right away, and your login is removed from Firebase Authentication. Any residual copies in Google’s systems are removed according to Google’s retention practices.
  • On your device, data stays in your browser until you clear it. Signing out removes your tasks and session history from that browser.
  • Deleted tasks leave a small deletion marker (task ID and time) for up to 60 days, so they don’t reappear from your other devices.

8. Your rights and choices

  • Access and portability: Settings → Account & sync → Export my data downloads everything as a JSON file.
  • Correction: edit your tasks and settings directly in the app.
  • Deletion: Settings → Account & sync → Delete account permanently deletes your account and synced data. Without an account, clear your browser’s site data.
  • Withdraw consent: disconnect Google Calendar at any time.

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR/UK GDPR, or California’s CCPA/CPRA), you may also have rights to object to or restrict processing, and to complain to your data-protection authority. We honor these rights for everyone. We do not sell or “share” personal information as those laws define it, and we do not use sensitive personal information.

9. Children

Aura is not directed to children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. You must be 13 or older to create an account, and if the law where you live sets a higher age for consenting to online services, you need a parent’s or guardian’s permission. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we’ll delete it.

10. Security

Aura is served only over HTTPS. Account data is protected by Google’s infrastructure and by database rules that allow each signed-in user to access only their own data. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password for your email or Google account.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we’ll update the date at the top. For significant changes affecting account holders, we’ll give notice in the app before they take effect.

12. Contact

Questions or privacy requests: open an issue at github.com/Logan-Huang/Pomodoro-Study-Timer/issues. Issues are public, so please don’t include personal details there. Say you’d like to be contacted privately, and include no more than is needed. Most requests (export, deletion) can be done instantly in the app.
